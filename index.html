Methodology & Thresholds

Weighted overlay: Score (0–100) = Σ(weight × class score) / 4 × 100. Each criterion scored 0 (Not suitable) to 4 (Highly suitable). Full citation list: FAO Land & Water Citrus sheet, FAO EcoCrop, NARC Dhankuta National Citrus Research Program annual reports, and peer-reviewed citrus land-suitability studies.

Prototype weights — require expert validation (not AHP pairwise-comparison derived).

NDVI/NDMI are shown as supporting vegetation-condition context only and are excluded from the weighted score — they do not determine whether citrus can grow, and are not evidence of disease/pathogen presence.

Prototype result based on publicly available and, where noted, synthetic placeholder environmental datasets (elevation, climate, soil, NDVI). District boundary is real. Field validation and local expert validation are required before using this for commercial planting decisions.
